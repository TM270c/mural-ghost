Ghost your mural onto the wall.

Photograph the wall, drop your design over the photo, then point the phone at the wall and the design floats on it at whatever opacity you like.

  1. Wall — stand where you'll paint from and snap the whole area with some edges around it.
  2. Align — drag, pinch and pull the corners of your design until it sits on the wall.
  3. Paint — the phone tracks the wall, and keeps tracking close up because it also learns four close-up crops of your photo.
